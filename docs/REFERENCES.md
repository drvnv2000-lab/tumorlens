# References to ground the study

Re-check versions, reporting checklists and recent work before manuscript submission. The short summaries below are for project scoping, not a substitute for a systematic review.

1. Suter Y, et al. **The LUMIERE dataset: Longitudinal glioblastoma MRI with expert RANO evaluation.** *Scientific Data*. 2022. [Data descriptor](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-022-01881-7). Describes 91 patients and expert radiographic ratings with free-text rationale; the dataset terms state non-commercial use.
2. Suter Y, et al. **LUMIERE dataset preparation code.** [Official GitHub repository](https://github.com/ysuter/gbm-data-longitudinal). Documents week conversion relative to the first pre-operative scan and notes that timing is floor-divided into weeks.
3. Suter Y, et al. **Automated longitudinal tumor measurement in glioblastoma patients using deep learning-based segmentation methods.** *Frontiers in Radiology*. 2023. [Article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10513769/). Closest prior LUMIERE longitudinal measurement analysis; makes reproducibility and manual correction relevant concerns.
4. Wen PY, et al. **RANO 2.0: Update to the Response Assessment in Neuro-Oncology Criteria for High- and Low-Grade Gliomas in Adults.** *Journal of Clinical Oncology*. 2023/2024. [Article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10860967/). Defines response criteria and contextual requirements; this package implements threshold arithmetic only.
5. Mongan J, et al. **Checklist for Artificial Intelligence in Medical Imaging (CLAIM): 2024 Update.** *Radiology: Artificial Intelligence*. 2024. [Article](https://pubs.rsna.org/doi/10.1148/ryai.240300). Reporting guidance for medical-imaging AI studies.
6. Collins GS, et al. **TRIPOD+AI statement: updated guidance for reporting clinical prediction models that use regression or machine learning methods.** *BMJ*. 2024. [Article](https://www.bmj.com/content/385/bmj-2023-078378). Apply if the work develops or evaluates a prediction model.
7. Vasey B, et al. **DECIDE-AI: reporting guideline for the early-stage clinical evaluation of decision support systems driven by AI.** *Nature Medicine*. 2022. [Article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-022-01772-9). Relevant only if the work later moves to live clinical evaluation.
8. Asfaw ZK, et al. **Artificial intelligence-based volumetric measurements for longitudinal clinical assessment of treatment response in high-grade gliomas: Validation across institutional and public datasets.** *Neuro-Oncology Advances*. 2026. [Article](https://academic.oup.com/noa/article/8/1/vdag045/8489052). Recent work directly raises novelty and comparator questions for volumetric response assessment.
9. Tarek A, et al. **TRACE: A Concept Bottleneck Model for Longitudinal 3D Glioblastoma Response Assessment.** 2026 preprint, accepted to the EXPLIMED workshop at IJCAI 2026 according to its arXiv record. [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.30313). Emerging overlapping work; its status and methods should be checked during a formal novelty review.

The proposed contribution must be framed around the exact question actually evaluated (measurement disagreement versus threshold proximity for selective deferral), and only if the adjudicated cohort and uncertainty analysis support it. Do not claim “first,” clinical safety, external validation, or superiority without direct evidence.

10. Leclercq AG, Moreau NN, et al. **CFB-GBM v2.0: An Augmented Longitudinal Dataset for Multi-Modal Glioblastoma Segmentation, Radiomics, and RANO Progression Tracking.** *Machine Learning for Biomedical Imaging*. 2026;3:763–771. [Article](https://doi.org/10.59275/j.melba.2026-dd48). Describes the 264-patient cohort, generated longitudinal GTVs, contour validation, and dataset-derived volumetric response labels.
