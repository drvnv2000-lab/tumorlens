# CFB-GBM selective transfer status

> **Archived (pre-v1).** CFB-GBM was a candidate cohort that was not pursued; v1 uses LUMIERE. Kept for the audit trail. Current results: [`V1_RESULTS.md`](V1_RESULTS.md). Index of all documents: [`README.md`](README.md).

**Checked:** 2026-10-11

The exact frozen selection for CFB-GBM Version 4 is present under `data/raw/cfb_gbm/imaging/CFB-GBM/`: 449 files across 165 pseudonymous subjects, comprising 437 GTV masks and 12 T1-Gd QC images. Validation found zero missing and zero unexpected files; total size is approximately 0.56 GB. The collection-wide 208 GB package was not downloaded.

The visual overlays were reviewed by the AI agent only. Human visual confirmation remains required before submission-level imaging analysis. No mask perturbation analysis completed: the threshold-flip run was interrupted before writing outcomes after the endpoint was reconsidered. See [the study decision](CFB_GBM_STUDY_DECISION.md).

Dataset citation: Moreau et al. (2025), *Pre and post treatment MRI and radiotherapy plans of patients with glioblastoma: the CFB-GBM cohort* (Version 4), TCIA. https://doi.org/10.7937/v9pn-2f72. License shown by TCIA: CC BY 4.0.
