# Data in this folder

Derived from the LUMIERE dataset (Suter et al., Scientific Data 9, 768, 2022; https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.c.5904905.v1).

## Licence

The data descriptor states that the dataset may only be used for non-commercial purposes, while the Figshare record lists CC0. TumorLens follows the stricter, non-commercial reading. These files are NOT covered by the repository's MIT licence. Not for clinical use.

## What is published, and what is not (provenance and disclosure review, 2026-10-12)

- Contents: 15 follow-up series (5 chosen to illustrate the results, 10 drawn at random with seed 20261011 from 81 other analysed series). For each visit there is one 2D post-contrast T1 slice at the level of the measured lesion, plus a mask image with the two tools' enhancing labels on that slice. These are derived research visualisations, not original clinical images; no volumes are published.
- Identifiers: only LUMIERE's own public patient and week identifiers (for example `Patient-051`, `week-064`). There are no dates, names, ages or other clinical fields, and no free-text expert rationale.
- Numbers: the expert's call and the dimensions parsed from the expert's text, plus each tool's diameters, volume and calls at each boundary offset.
- Images: PNG files with no embedded metadata (checked: no text chunks). Slices are skull-stripped as released. Skull stripping does not by itself guarantee anonymity, but this release is already public and de-identified by its authors, and only single 2D slices are shown here.
- Generated by `scripts/build_viewer_cases.py`.
