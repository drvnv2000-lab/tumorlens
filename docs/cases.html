Fifteen real follow-up series, measured twice

Each scan is a contrast-enhanced T1 slice from LUMIERE at the level where the automated lesion is largest. Two released segmentations, HD-GLIO-AUTO and DeepBraTumIA, are measured the RANO way and turned into calls. The neuroradiologist's call is shown beside them. Calls are measurement-driven: RANO size thresholds against the nadir, with no confirmation scan, steroids, clinical status or non-enhancing disease. The expert call uses all of those. Not a medical device.

What you are looking at

"Illustrative" cases were chosen to show the patterns in the v1 results. "Random" cases were drawn with a fixed seed from the other analysed series, without looking at their calls. Across 350 automated calls, a uniform 1 mm edge offset changes 17% of HD-GLIO-AUTO calls and 28% of DeepBraTumIA calls under the study's call rules. A 0.5 mm offset is smaller than most voxels and changes almost nothing.

The outline is the tool's enhancing label on this slice. Calipers appear only for a tool whose largest lesion is measured on the slice shown. The baseline is the expert's post-operative scan. HD-GLIO-AUTO masks for scans stored in PIR orientation were rotated 180 degrees in the release; they are shown corrected.
